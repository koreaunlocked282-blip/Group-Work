    How it works. Three steps.

    01 Anonymous

    Share your experience

    Choose a category, an area and what happened. You can report without an account. Do not include names or phone numbers.

    02 Checked

    Safety check before publishing

    A public version is checked automatically for people's names, phone numbers and business details. If anything is found, it is held for review and not shown.

    03 Public

    Patterns stay searchable

    Only stories whose authors choose to share become public. They can be filtered by category and province, and the map shows how many stories each area has.

    Public reports cannot contain names or phone numbers. Before a public version is shared, an automatic check looks for people's names, phone numbers and business details. If it finds any, you see what was found and a cleaned version, and you cannot continue until the text is clean. The check runs again on our server, so a public version that still contains them is held for review and not shown. Places and locations may stay.

    FAQ / Safety notes

    Questions.

    Clear answers before you put anything on the public record.

    01Is my report really anonymous?

    Yes. You can report without an account or contact details, and you get a case code and a tracking secret instead. Public versions never include your name. Only a hash of your tracking secret is stored, and network addresses are used only in scrambled form to limit abuse.

    02Can I name the person who treated me unfairly?

    No. Public versions cannot contain names or phone numbers. An automatic check looks for people's names, phone numbers and business details before anything is shown. Describe what happened and where in general terms. Places may stay.

    03Is this an official complaint?

    No. Korea Unlocked is a free student project, not an official complaint channel, and a story here is not proof of any allegation. For emergencies call 112 or 119, and for immigration questions call 1345.

    04Can I take my story down?

    Yes. If you shared a public story, open Track an existing case with your case code and tracking secret, or My reports if you have an account, and use the withdraw button. It stops being shown. Deleting all your data is not available yet.
